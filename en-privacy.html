2. Saves and age range

PELPU stores saved games, preferences, high scores, statistics, competition attempts and best scores on your device. There is no PELPU account or developer-operated score server. We do not automatically receive these local records. PELPU includes no additional developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service.

The age choice stores only a category: under 13, 13–15, 16–17 or 18 and older. We do not request a birthday. Normal play works without this information; Daily, Weekly and Monthly require a choice. It is saved and not requested again on subsequent launches. Settings let you increase protection for younger players sharing the device.

Saves and preferences are necessary for the features you request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR; necessary device access under section 25(2)(2) German TDDDG). The age category supports our legitimate interest in age-appropriate access and protecting younger players (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). It is not required for normal play.

Local data remains until app data is deleted. Operating-system backups may retain copies, which you and Apple manage. Browser and iOS saves are separate. We cannot delete local data remotely.

3. Game Center

When you use Game Center, the app handles a game-specific player identifier and sends completed results to Apple. Your nickname, score and rank may appear on leaderboards according to your Game Center settings. Players under 13 use only local competition results in PELPU; the app does not sign them into Game Center or submit their scores.

Pending results and their account association remain locally until successful submission; expired competition results are discarded. Apple retains submitted leaderboard data under its own rules. The requested leaderboard feature relies, where applicable, on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Details and contacts: Game Center & Privacy.

4. Optional AdMob advertising

PELPU offers only optional ads for an extra life, with no automatic advertising banners. At most one rewarded extra life is available per round. Your score stays, the board restarts with twelve tiles and your streak resets. Weekly and Monthly may offer up to two ad videos; the app explains this beforehand. An ad click or purchase is not required.

Players under 16 receive no ad requests or ad extra lives. No ads are requested without an age choice either. For ages 16–17, Google teen treatment and restricted data processing are additionally enabled. All requested ads are non-personalized with general (G) content rating. PELPU does not request iOS tracking permission and disables Google publisher first-party IDs.

Non-personalized does not mean anonymous. Google may process IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad and interaction data, and diagnostic and performance information. These support ad delivery, measurement, billing and security. PELPU does not send your saved game or Game Center name as an advertising profile. See Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Before loading an ad, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks privacy status and presents a consent form where required. This involves technical connection data and your privacy decision, which is stored on the device. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, no ad is loaded without the necessary consent; PELPU does not fall back to consent-free limited ads after rejection. Normal play and available tournaments remain accessible. Selecting “Watch ad” does not replace consent.

Consent-dependent ad processing relies on Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and the associated device access on section 25(1) German TDDDG. Managing your privacy choice supports our obligations to document and respect it (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR). Use Settings → Ad privacy options to change or withdraw choices where this option is required for your location. Withdrawal applies going forward. An extra life is not guaranteed when permission, connectivity or an ad is unavailable.

AdMob is provided by Google, including Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland in the European context; Google LLC in the USA may also be involved. Under its published terms, Google generally processes advertising data as an independent controller. The consent form lists participating advertising technology partners and purposes. PELPU integrates no additional mediation SDKs. See Google on partner apps and Google data protection terms.

We keep a local receipt for an earned extra life to prevent duplicate awards in the same round. It is removed when obsolete round records are cleared. Google sets retention according to the data and purpose; security, fraud prevention and legal obligations may require longer retention. PELPU does not specify a single Google retention period. See Google retention information.

5. Support

When you contact pelpu@gmx.de, we use your sender address, message and voluntary attachments to handle your request. Please do not send passwords or payment details. Depending on the request, the basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR or our legitimate interest in answering inquiries under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

The mailbox uses GMX / 1&1 Mail & Media GmbH, Elgendorfer Straße 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany. The service processes message and connection data required for email operations. See the GMX privacy notice (German).

We normally delete resolved support inquiries within six months. Where statutory retention duties apply or records are required for specific legal claims or defence, we retain the necessary information until that reason ends.

6. International processing

Apple and Google may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the USA. Their notices describe the applicable adequacy decisions and, where relevant, standard contractual clauses. You can consult these safeguards and request further information through their privacy contacts: Google transfer frameworks and Apple Privacy Policy.

7. Your rights

Subject to legal conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and data portability. You may object to legitimate-interest processing on grounds relating to your particular situation. You may withdraw consent at any time for the future; prior lawful processing is unaffected.

Contact pelpu@gmx.de. Apple and Google also provide privacy contacts for data they independently manage. You may complain to a supervisory authority, particularly where you live or work. In Hamburg: Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information. PELPU does not use automated profiling to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.
