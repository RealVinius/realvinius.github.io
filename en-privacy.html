Privacy

Your data in PELPU: local saves, leaderboards and optional advertising.

Updated: 23 October 2026

A simple explanation for younger players

Your saved game stays on your device. From age 13, completed scores may appear on a Game Center leaderboard if you use it. You do not have to give your age to play normally. For tournaments, your age range helps us choose suitable settings; we do not ask for your birthday.

Watching an ad for an extra life is optional. Under 16, we use restricted child ads. Google still receives data, such as your IP address, and may use storage to detect fraud. You can keep playing without ads, but will not receive an ad extra life. If anything is unclear, you can ask a parent to help you write to pelpu@gmx.de.

2. Saves and age range

PELPU stores saved games, preferences, high scores, statistics, competition attempts and best scores on your device. There is no PELPU account or developer-operated score server. We do not automatically receive these local records. PELPU includes no additional developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service.

The age choice stores only a category: under 13, 13–15, 16–17 or 18 and older. We do not request a birthday. Normal play works without this information; Daily, Weekly and Monthly require a choice. It is saved and not requested again on subsequent launches. Settings let you increase protection for younger players sharing the device.

Saves and preferences are necessary for the features you request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR; necessary device access under section 25(2)(2) German TDDDG). The age category supports our legitimate interest in age-appropriate access and protecting younger players (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). It is not required for normal play.

Local data remains until app data is deleted. Operating-system backups may retain copies, which you and Apple manage. Browser and iOS saves are separate. We cannot delete local data remotely.

3. Game Center

When you use Game Center, the app handles a game-specific player identifier and sends completed results to Apple. Your nickname, score and rank may appear on leaderboards according to your Game Center settings. Players under 13 use only local competition results in PELPU; the app does not sign them into Game Center or submit their scores.

Pending results and their account association remain locally until successful submission; expired competition results are discarded. Apple retains submitted leaderboard data under its own rules. The requested leaderboard feature relies, where applicable, on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Details and contacts: Game Center & Privacy.

4. Optional ads and protection for younger players

Current status

The iOS app is being prepared for release. Its ad flow has been checked with Google test ads in the iOS simulator. The iOS app is intended to use the age-dependent advertising described below. The browser game preview does not serve AdMob ads. This notice describes the current pre-release version; the final privacy assessment for production release remains open.

An extra life is optional

You can play normally without watching ads. The prepared offer allows at most one ad extra life per round: your points stay, the board restarts with twelve tiles and your streak resets. Daily, Weekly and Monthly keep the same attempt. Weekly and Monthly may include up to two ad videos, explained before you choose. There are no automatic banners. Visiting an advertised site, installing something or making a purchase is not required. The extra life requires the earned-reward signal and ad dismissal. Early cancellation, failure or an unavailable ad does not guarantee an extra life. No free replacement extra life is offered.

How is your age used?

Without a saved age range, the app makes neither UMP privacy requests nor ad requests. For players under 16, the ad service is contacted only after the optional extra life is chosen. The tested child flow uses Google CHILD treatment, Limited Ads, restricted data processing and content rating G. It does not use an adult consent form or collect parental consent. A previous adult privacy decision is not treated as a child’s consent.

Ages 16–17 use Google TEEN treatment and restricted data processing. Ages 18+ use the standard age treatment. For every age range, PELPU requests non-personalized ads, disables publisher first-party IDs and does not request iOS tracking permission. The age category itself stays on the device; Google receives the resulting protection signals.

Google states that CHILD prevents transmission of the iOS advertising identifier IDFA and requests to third-party measurement pixels and ad servers. This does not mean that no data is processed. See Google age treatment.

What data is involved in ads?

Google needs connection data to deliver ads. Depending on the ad flow, the SDK may process IP address and approximate location, device and app information and technical device identifiers, ad requests and video interactions, and diagnostic and performance data. These may support delivery, measurement, billing and ad security. These are SDK-wide data categories; CHILD and Limited Ads restrict their use. PELPU does not send your saved game or Game Center name as an ad profile. See Google SDK data disclosures.

Limited Ads does not mean storage-free. Ads may be cached. Our AdMob account enables programmatic Limited Ads. Google documents the use of invalid-traffic-only cookies and local storage to detect invalid ad traffic and protect against fraud and abuse, including without a consent signal. Under its policies, Google does not require consent for this described use case. Sharing full IP addresses with buyers is disabled; Google still receives your IP address for the connection. See Google on Limited Ads and fraud-detection storage.

Consent and legal bases

For ages 16+, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks privacy status and displays a consent form where required. Google processes technical connection data and the privacy choice, which is saved on the device. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, PELPU requires the necessary consent, including storage and Google vendor consent, before advertising. Refusal or withdrawal does not switch you into the child flow. Choosing “Watch ad” does not replace consent. You can change or withdraw choices through Settings → Ad privacy options where this option is required for your location. Withdrawal applies going forward. Normal play and tournaments available for your profile remain accessible.

Consent-dependent advertising relies on Article 6(1)(a) GDPR; corresponding device access relies on section 25(1) German TDDDG. Managing privacy choices supports documenting and respecting them (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR). The CHILD flow is configured using Google’s published guidance for limited ads and does not obtain parental consent. Google’s statement about fraud-detection storage is a provider statement, not regulatory approval. The legal basis for remaining device access and processing in this flow still needs a final assessment before production release.

Recipients and retention

Google provides AdMob and UMP, including Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland in the European context; Google LLC in the USA may also be involved. Google is responsible for its own processing; we remain responsible for its integration into PELPU, our disclosures and any consent required. The specific allocation of roles depends on the service, processing and applicable law. See the Google advertising data protection terms and Google EU user consent policy. PELPU adds no other mediation SDKs. The Google consent form lists available ad technology partners and purposes for the ages-16+ consent flow; this list is not equivalent to the restricted child flow. See Google on partner apps.

A local reward receipt prevents duplicate extra lives and is cleared with obsolete round records. Google determines its retention by data type, purpose and legal requirements. The linked documentation does not specify a fixed storage period for our iOS CHILD/Limited Ads flow. Google’s general criteria and deletion options are described in its retention information.

Questions from children and parents

You or your parents can contact pelpu@gmx.de, including to request access, correction or deletion. Please do not initially send an identity document, exact birthday or passwords. If we need to verify a record or parental authority to handle a request, we will ask only for the necessary information. We do not receive a copy of your local saved game. We can help identify the right contact for data Google manages independently; contacting PELPU does not automatically delete that data. The rights in section 7 also apply to children.

5. Support

When you contact pelpu@gmx.de, we use your sender address, message and voluntary attachments to handle your request. Please do not send passwords or payment details. Depending on the request, the basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR or our legitimate interest in answering inquiries under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

The mailbox uses GMX / 1&1 Mail & Media GmbH, Elgendorfer Straße 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany. The service processes message and connection data required for email operations. See the GMX privacy notice (German).

We normally delete resolved support inquiries within six months. Where statutory retention duties apply or records are required for specific legal claims or defence, we retain the necessary information until that reason ends.

6. International processing

Apple and Google may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the USA. Their notices describe the applicable adequacy decisions and, where relevant, standard contractual clauses. You can consult these safeguards and request further information through their privacy contacts: Google transfer frameworks and Apple Privacy Policy.

7. Your rights

Subject to legal conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and data portability. You may object to legitimate-interest processing on grounds relating to your particular situation. You may withdraw consent at any time for the future; prior lawful processing is unaffected.

Contact pelpu@gmx.de. Apple and Google also provide privacy contacts for data they independently manage. You may complain to a supervisory authority, particularly where you live or work. In Hamburg: Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information. PELPU does not use automated profiling to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.
