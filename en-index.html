Common questions

How does PELPU work?

Swipe to move every tile in one direction. Connect at least four matching tiles horizontally or vertically. Further clears in the same move make a combo. Clears on consecutive moves build a streak.

Where is my saved game?

Normal rounds are saved on this device and can be opened with Resume. Reinstalling the app or deleting app data may remove progress. The browser and iOS app have separate saves. PELPU does not sync saves between devices.

When do attempts reset?

Daily: three attempts a day. Weekly: three a week. Monthly: ten per calendar month. Each mode has its own counter on this device. Resets happen at 00:00 UTC: every day for Daily, Monday for Weekly, and the first of the month for Monthly. Your local time may differ. The in-game countdown shows the time remaining.

Can I resume a competition attempt?

A Daily, Weekly or Monthly attempt cannot be resumed after returning to the menu or restarting the app. PELPU asks before you leave through the menu. The attempt is then spent. Your normal saved round is kept separately.

How does the extra life work?

The prepared iOS app offers one optional ad extra life per round. A restricted under-16 flow has been checked with test ads; live child ads have not yet been cleared for production release. Ages 16+ retain the required consent choices. You keep your points, restart the board with twelve tiles and reset your streak. In competitions, this is still the same attempt. An extra life cannot be guaranteed when no ad is available. The browser preview lets you test the feature without a real ad.

Why is my rank missing?

Online leaderboards require the iOS app, Game Center and an internet connection. Connect Game Center before starting a competition attempt. Your best completed score counts and must be submitted before the period ends. The browser preview has no global leaderboard. Online services will be configured and tested before launch.

How can I report an ad?

Include the approximate time and advertiser if possible. A screenshot can help. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other confidential information.

Children and privacy

Normal play works without an age choice. Tournaments save an age range once on the device. Under-13 competition results stay local. Our privacy notice explains the prepared child advertising flow and its release status.
